About my photography

Photography snuck up on me. I didn't set out to be a photographer — I just started noticing things I didn't want to forget, and my phone was always in my pocket.


What I love most is the edit. Taking the photo is just the beginning. I can spend an hour in Lightroom on a single frame, adjusting the light until it feels exactly like how the moment actually felt — not just how it looked.


I shoot street scenes, quiet corners of Tokyo, food, people, and a lot of sky.
