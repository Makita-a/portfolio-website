About my art
✦ Minor: Intermedia Art and Sciences — Waseda University

Art has always been an important part of my life — and that passion is exactly why I chose Intermedia Art and Sciences as a minor at Waseda University.


I enjoy exploring how technology, design, creativity, and human expression intersect. Through courses like Contemporary Art Theory, Fundamentals of Visual Expression, and both Intermedia Art and Science Labs, I've learned to think differently and approach problems from multiple angles.


Art taught me the balance between technical and creative thinking — something that turns out to be incredibly useful in engineering and research too. The best solutions are rarely just logical. They're also beautiful.
